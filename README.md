## 🧠 Figures, Analysts & Traders

### Figures & Analysts

- [Car](https://x.com/CarOnPolymarket) — Whale-spotting and Polymarket-ecosystem commentary — volume ATHs, platform history, high-profile disputed bets. (Reportedly lost a Polymarket verification badge at one point — read as independent commentary, not platform-affiliated.)
- [Archive](https://x.com/ArchiveExplorer) — Occasional deep-dive threads on market mechanics (e.g. multi-outcome arbitrage setups) mixed in with general viral-story content. (Not a dedicated prediction-market account — and there's a near-identical handle, @ArchivExplorer, worth not confusing with this one.)
- [Goaty](https://x.com/goatyishere) — Story-style breakdowns of notable Polymarket whale wallets and unusual trades. (Own trading activity is minimal per their Polymarket profile — this is commentary, not a personal track record; also promotes third-party tools.)
- [kober](https://x.com/kober1337) — Co-builder of PolyScalping (a Polymarket reward-tracking tool) and posts scalping/arbitrage educational content.
- [Danko](https://x.com/DankoWeb3) — Occasional threads on prediction-market copy-trading infrastructure. (Most content is general crypto/Web3, not prediction-market-specific — borderline fit.)
- [Aggie](https://x.com/BlondiePredicts) — Prediction-market-focused commentary and a companion Substack, framing markets as "the new social media."
- [John Wang](https://x.com/j0hnwang) — Was a crypto-influencer voice on Kalshi-vs-Polymarket dynamics; now Kalshi's own Head of Crypto, so treat recent takes as company commentary, not independent analysis.
- [Farokh](https://x.com/farokh) — Rug Radio founder and long-running crypto/NFT podcast host. (No confirmed prediction-market-specific content found — crypto-adjacent, not a dedicated voice.)
- [Tarek Mansour](https://x.com/mansourtarek_) — Kalshi's CEO and co-founder, posting on funding, regulation, and platform strategy straight from leadership.
- [Dan Schwarz](https://x.com/dschwarz26) — CEO of FutureSearch, an AI-forecasting company building models to predict Polymarket questions; writes on prediction-market research.
- [Primo Data](https://x.com/primo_data) — Independent data/analytics builder in the Polymarket ecosystem, covering APIs and on-chain data resources.
- [Shayne Coplan](https://x.com/shayne_coplan) — Polymarket's founder and CEO.
- [gemchanger](https://x.com/gemchange_ltd) — General crypto-trading commentary with occasional, incidental Polymarket mentions. (Not a dedicated prediction-market account — the weakest fit here, worth a second look.)
- [Saurav](https://x.com/saurav_tweets) — Breaks down specific mispriced markets with the underlying math — e.g. arguing a Polymarket "will Sinner hit 15,000 ATP points" market was underpriced at 18% given the ranking-points math for the rest of the season, then followed up with the market link and chart.

## 🏛️ Platforms

- [PredictIt](https://x.com/PredictIt) — Official account of PredictIt, the CFTC no-action political/event market. (Not to be confused with the unrelated @PredictIt_.)
- [Metaculus](https://x.com/metaculus) — Official account of Metaculus, the community forecasting-aggregation platform. (No real-money trading — a forecasting site, not an exchange.)
- [Kalshi](https://x.com/Kalshi) — Official account of Kalshi, the CFTC-regulated U.S. event-contracts exchange.
  - [Kalshi Sports](https://x.com/KalshiSports) — Sports-vertical feed, part of Kalshi's branded family of topic-specific accounts. (Bio couldn't be independently confirmed as in-house — worth a quick manual check before treating as fully official.)
  - [Kalshi Football](https://x.com/KalshiGridiron) — American football-focused feed carrying Kalshi branding. (Official status unconfirmed — signs point toward a third-party/affiliate account rather than in-house: near-duplicate accounts with similar branding exist, some content is off-topic for a market feed, and Kalshi has previously pulled affiliate badges from accounts like this after hate-speech incidents. Verify before treating as official.)
  - [Kalshi Footy](https://x.com/KalshiFooty) — Soccer-focused feed carrying Kalshi branding. (Same caveat as Kalshi Football — official status unconfirmed, evidence leans third-party/affiliate rather than in-house.)
  - [Kalshi Crypto](https://x.com/Kalshi_Crypto) — Posts Kalshi crypto-market content. (Not listed among Kalshi's own confirmed official channels — @Kalshi and @KalshiSupport are the only ones Kalshi's own site names. Official status unconfirmed.)
- [Polymarket](https://x.com/Polymarket) — Official account of Polymarket, the largest crypto-based prediction market by volume.
  - [Polymarket Money](https://x.com/PolymarketMoney) — Whale/large-trade flow tracking for Polymarket. (Not listed among Polymarket's own official channels — appears to be an independent, third-party tracker despite the name.)
  - [Polymarket Traders](https://x.com/PolymarketTrade) — Trader-leaderboard and search tooling built on Polymarket's 1M+-trader dataset. (Also not on Polymarket's official channel list — third-party, not platform-run.)
- [Limitless](https://x.com/trylimitless) — Official account of Limitless, the largest short-duration (hourly/daily) prediction market, built on Base.
- [Opinion](https://x.com/opinionlabsxyz) — Official account of Opinion, a YZi Labs-backed prediction market and oracle-infrastructure platform.
- [predict.fun](https://x.com/predictdotfun) — Official account of predict.fun, a YZi Labs-backed prediction market building on BNB Chain.

## 📰 News & Media

- [Eventual](https://eventual.news/) — Prediction-markets media startup: daily articles, newsletters, twice-weekly live video shows, and podcasts with traders, founders, and policymakers. Founded by Alex Keeney; backed by LightShed Ventures, Riverside Ventures, Juniper Ventures, and Bryan Goldberg. Polymarket is its launch sponsor and data partner. X: [@EventualNews](https://x.com/EventualNews).
- [The Oracle by Polymarket](https://news.polymarket.com/) — Polymarket's in-house editorial arm: a Substack (90K+ subscribers), a podcast, and a YouTube channel covering forecasting education and market analysis. X: [@polymarketinfo](https://x.com/polymarketinfo) · YouTube: [@OracleByPolymarket](https://www.youtube.com/@OracleByPolymarket).
- [Sports Illustrated — Prediction Markets](https://www.si.com/prediction-markets) — SI's dedicated vertical covering prediction-market odds and movements, mostly sports-focused (NFL, NBA, MLB, college sports, UFC) across Kalshi, Polymarket, and other platforms. (Mixes genuine market analysis with promotional bonus-code/sportsbook-offer content — read the two apart.)
